About

A small, honest toolbox

Toolshed is seven single-purpose calculators — a tip splitter, a percentage calculator, a BMI check, a unit converter, an age calculator, a word counter, and a password generator. That's it. No account, no app to install, nothing saved on a server.

Every calculation runs in your browser with plain JavaScript. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Why it looks like a blueprint

Because these are measuring instruments, not a lifestyle brand. The grid, the figure numbers, and the gauge in the header are meant to feel like a page out of a drafting notebook — a place for numbers to be exact, not decorated.
